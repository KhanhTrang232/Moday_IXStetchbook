Lab Reflection

In this lab, I used absolute positioning to place the four stars in different areas of the night sky. I used scale, rotate, translate, and skew to create a different hover effect for each star. I also combined more than one transform on some stars, so they could change size, move, and rotate during the same interaction.

The transition-duration property made the hover effects happen more smoothly instead of changing suddenly. Absolute positioning gave me more control over where each star appeared, although I had to adjust the positions and sizes so the stars would not overlap with the moon.

I also changed the moon's box-shadow on hover to move the glow to the opposite side. After experimenting, I adjusted the star sizes and positions and added responsive sizing for smaller screens. This activity helped me understand how positioning, transforms, and transitions work together in CSS.
