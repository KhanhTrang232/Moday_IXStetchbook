Joplin,

Wishing

Can one last wish set her free?

Sometimes a wish begins quietly.

Joplin looked out at the world and wondered what might happen if everything could change. She had always believed that wishing was something people did when they were afraid to act, but lately she was beginning to think differently. Maybe a wish could be the first step toward becoming the person she wanted to be.

She wanted something more.

The future felt close enough to imagine, but too far away to touch.
She kept thinking about all the places she had never seen and all the things she had never tried. The more she thought about them, the more she realized that waiting would not make her dreams happen.

THE POSSIBILITY OF CHANGE

Joplin took a breath and allowed herself to imagine a different life. For the first time, the uncertainty did not feel completely frightening. It felt like an opportunity.

Process Reflection

Before this assignment, I thought of hierarchy mostly as a way to organize information. After working with HTML, I realized that hierarchy can also change how people understand a text. I used different heading levels, bold text, and italic text to make some words feel more important than others.

Instead of reading the passage from beginning to end in a normal way, the reader's attention is pulled toward different parts of the page. The headings make certain ideas feel bigger, while the smaller headings make other ideas feel less important. Using strong and em also changes the tone because those words stand out from the rest of the paragraph.

I think this helped me understand how designers can control what people notice first. Even though the words stay mostly the same, changing their visual hierarchy can make the meaning feel different. If I had more time, I would experiment with even more unusual ways to arrange the text and create more visual chaos.
