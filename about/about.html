A Little About Me

Hi, my name is Katie. I am originally from Vietnam and I am currently studying at San Francisco State University. I am interested in creative work, especially movies, visual design, music, and storytelling. I enjoy watching films and paying attention to the way directors use color, composition, and small details to create a certain feeling. I also like journaling because it gives me a way to organize my thoughts and express myself creatively.

I am taking this class because I want to learn more about how websites work and how design and creativity can be combined with technology. Before this class, I did not know much about HTML, so I am interested in learning how to build and organize my own webpages. I hope to become more comfortable with coding and develop my own creative style through these projects. One of my favorite creative resources is YouTube, where I often find movies, music, creative videos, and inspiration from other people.

I am still learning and experimenting, so I do not expect everything I create to be perfect. I want my website to show my interests and personality while also giving me a chance to try new things.


Process Reflection

Creating this About page helped me understand how different parts of a webpage can work together. I learned how to organize information using headings and paragraphs and how to add an external hyperlink using the anchor tag. I also learned more about embedding content from another website. At first, the iframe code looked confusing because there were several different attributes inside it. After looking at the code more closely, I understood that the src tells the browser where the video comes from and the title describes the embedded content.

For my video self-portrait, I wanted to keep the idea simple instead of trying to make a professional video. I chose a self-portrait that represents some of my interests and personality. Making a video also made me think differently about an About page because I could introduce myself through movement and visuals instead of only using words. When I wrote my biography, I decided to focus on where I am from, what I enjoy, and why I am taking this class. I left out information that was not connected to the purpose of the page. With more time, I would experiment with the layout and design of the page and make the video feel more connected to the rest of the website.
