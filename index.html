My Website

Earlier Process Reflection

For this entry page, I focused on creating a simple structure that helps visitors understand what they can find on my website. One idea from the reading that I used is that information architecture is the structure behind a website, while navigation helps visitors move through that structure. I organized my website into four main pages: Home, About, Inspiration, and Self Portrait. I put these pages in the navigation so visitors can easily move between them.

I also created a simple site map before building the page. The sketch helped me see how the pages connect and which page should be the main entry point. I decided to keep the structure simple because my website is still growing. The entry page introduces the site and gives visitors a clear idea of what they can explore. I also used headings and sections to create a basic visual hierarchy, with the main title first and smaller sections underneath it.

One thing I am still thinking about is how the navigation might change when I add more pages. If the website becomes larger, I may need to organize the pages into categories instead of putting everything in one navigation bar. For now, I think this simple structure makes the site easy to understand and gives me a good starting point for future revisions.
